## 题型结构（固定）

```
Part I Writing (30 minutes)

Directions: ... write an essay that begins with the sentence "______".
You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences
to develop your essay.
You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words.
```

**唯一任务：** 解释首句为什么重要 → 给出 2 个理由或例子 → 回到大学生/青年提出行动建议。
