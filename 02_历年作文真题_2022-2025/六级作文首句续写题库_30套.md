# 大学英语六级作文首句续写题库（2022.06–2025.12）

> **收录说明**：本文件收录 2022 年 6 月至 2025 年 12 月期间全部 30 套六级作文真题。自 2022.06 起，六级作文已连续 8 个场次锁死"给定首句续写"题型——考生须抄写题目给出的完整首句，围绕其展开 150–200 词的评论短文。本文件已对 OCR 扫描产生的识别噪声做语义修正，仅保留纯净的 Directions 与给定首句。
>
> **修正说明**：原始扫描件中存在以下典型 OCR 错误，已逐一修正：`students5` → `students'`、`selAworth` → `self-worth`、`MNowadays` → `Nowadays`、`giypn` → `given`、`yoor` → `your`、句末噪声 `95/99/w` → 英文引号 `"`。

## 2025 年 12 月

### <a id="202512-第1套"></a>第 1 套

**Directions:** For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence *"Living in an increasingly competitive world, college students should better prepare themselves to confront various challenges."* You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words. You should copy the sentence given in quotes at the beginning of your essay.

**给定首句：** *Living in an increasingly competitive world, college students should better prepare themselves to confront various challenges.*

**主题：** 竞争世界中的大学生挑战应对
